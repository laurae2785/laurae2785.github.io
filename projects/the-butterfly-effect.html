The Butterfly Effect Project

Context:

This project was for one of my SEP10 lessons in which we took previously learned content into a challenge by making a movie poster/webpage, concepts for the making included HTML & CSS. Using those me and my partner created our own story and added fonts,colors,borders and more. At the end, we decided to make it suspenseful movie about time traveling.

Content:

Before we started making our project, we dedided who was going to be responsable for each part and made a plan to ensure nothing would get mixed up. Afterwards, we agreed on the topic for our page. In summary, it's about a guy named Samuel who's wife passed away on a car accident. Unexpectedly, he finds her time machine and makes multiple attemps to prevent her death. Eventually, he makes a terrible choice and ends up stuck in time. We wanted our story to be mysterious and fictional so we picked that story.

Reflection:

While making our project, me and my partner faced one major challenge that was not being able to center images. At first we were confused since we thought we followed everything we had learned in class, we tried different methods we found on Google but nothing seemed to work out. Later on, we asked our teacher for help and he explained that we had to select the .bigdiv element instead of the class element we were using for each individual image. This was helpful because in the future i will remember to keep things organized and know in where each thing/image is placed so that similar problems won't happen or will be easier to fix.
